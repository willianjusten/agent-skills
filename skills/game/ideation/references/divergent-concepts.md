# Divergent concepts

Goal: avoid locking onto the first idea.

1. Restate the spark in one sentence.
2. Offer two or three concepts that differ on at least two axes: player fantasy, core verb, camera or perspective, session length, tone.
3. Give each a name, a one-line pitch and the feeling it sells. No mechanics lists.
4. Recommend one and say why. Allow a hybrid only if it keeps one clear verb.
5. Record the unpicked concepts, one line each, as rejected.

Test: if two concepts could share a core loop, they are not divergent; replace one.
