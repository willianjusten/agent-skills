# <Title> treatment

Agents read this before touching code. Keep it to one screen.

## Idea

One or two sentences: what it is and what it should make the viewer or player feel.

## Palette

Hex values, each with its job (background, subject, accents, hazards). Three to six colors.

## Camera

Lens feel, position, whether it moves, and how.

## Input mapping

Keys, pointer and touch, and what each does. Say what happens with no input.

## Not this

Looks and behaviors to avoid, so agents do not drift to defaults. Name at least five, each concrete ("no neon purple gradients", "no screen shake on every hit").
